# Readiness and Approval

## Verification

- [ ] Clear and unambiguous.
- [ ] Internally consistent and feasible.
- [ ] Tailored BRS coverage is complete or exclusions have rationale.
- [ ] Every material item has a source or explicit rationale.
- [ ] Business, stakeholder, solution, transition, and design information are classified correctly.
- [ ] Terms and identifiers are consistent.
- [ ] No hidden design is presented as business intent.
- [ ] Backward and forward traceability is sufficient for change analysis.

## Validation

- [ ] Business owner confirms the need and expected value.
- [ ] Scope and non-goals are suitable.
- [ ] Capability map covers in-scope target business behavior.
- [ ] Rules and constraints have the required authority.
- [ ] Success measures and evidence sources are adequate.
- [ ] Blocking conflicts are resolved; remaining questions have owners and dates.
